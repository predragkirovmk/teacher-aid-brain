# 2026-09-16

Integration, a feasibility read on actually building it, and a full pass over the vault.

## Today

- **Integrated [[feature-spec-v1]] across 13 notes.** Every decision from yesterday now
  reaches the notes it contradicted. The two that matter most are the spoken ones: the
  focus-lock answer and the "never grades anyone" answer were both false and are rewritten,
  each carrying a dated note saying not to give the old version.
- **Assessed whether v1 can actually be built** — [[feasibility]]. Verdict: buildable, about
  ninety per cent of the code writable in Claude Code sessions, **110–165 sessions total**,
  41–59 of them to a first real lesson. Three boundaries that are not about code: Next.js
  cannot reach the app stores alone, iOS focus lock depends on Apple granting the Family
  Controls entitlement, and focus lock cannot be verified without a physical device.
- **Decided Android first.** Mandatory focus lock, no dev accounts and "one real lesson ASAP"
  cannot all hold on iOS — the entity → D-U-N-S → Apple → entitlement chain is six to twelve
  weeks. Android has no gate: sideload an APK, usage access works with no Google approval. So
  the lock stays mandatory and real from day one, and iOS follows when Apple answers.
- **Created [[build-v1]]** with [[feasibility]] and [[milestones]].

    year one, and the handbook said both. Now counted in the market, explicitly not served by

    ~EUR 95,500 because a target should not count institutions the product cannot serve.
  - "Five euros per teacher per month" was still in the handbook's lines-never-cut, three days
    after the price became EUR 199 flat.
  - [[value-propositions]] still said per-seat pricing.
  - [[prototype]] called the repo private; it went public on 13 Sep with the host key rotated.
  - Time-relative phrasing stranded in Area notes — "this weekend", "recruit one this week".
- **Archived Startup Weekend**, three days late by the vault's own rule. Lifted the six
  durable objection answers into [[objections]], rewritten for headmasters rather than judges.
