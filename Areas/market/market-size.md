# Market size

**Updated 28 Sep 2026.** TAM / SAM / SOM is now drawn geographically, not by school level:
**TAM = global, SAM = Europe, SOM = North Macedonia.** Priced at **EUR 9 per student per
year**, the price on the current Canva deck (slide 28: "9 euro per student"; confirmed as
*per year* on 28 Sep). This replaces the 13 Sep cut, which was Macedonia-only at EUR 199 a
month flat per school. See [[#Superseded — MK-only, EUR 199/month per institution (13 Sep 2026)]].

## TAM / SAM / SOM — Global / Europe / Macedonia

The segment is the same at every level and the same client as before: **upper-secondary
students (ISCED 3) + university students (ISCED 5–8).** Primary and lower secondary are
excluded. In MK, lower secondary is part of the nine-year primary school, so ISCED 3 is
exactly the гимназија / стручно / уметничко school the product is built for.

| | Geography | Students | ARR at EUR 9 / student / year | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | Global | **~553 M** | **~EUR 5.0 B** | UNESCO UIS, 2023: 288.5 M upper secondary + 265.0 M tertiary |
| **SAM** | Europe (definition below) | **~46.7 M** | **~EUR 420 M** | Eurostat 2024 (`educ_uoe_enra01`); UK from UNESCO UIS 2023 |
| **SOM** | North Macedonia | **~124,400** | **~EUR 1.1 M** | Eurostat 2024: 67,660 upper secondary + 56,761 tertiary |

- **Year-3 target inside the SOM: about a third of Macedonia.** That is about 41,000
  students, or about EUR 370k a year. This is an estimate: the "a third of the country in
  three years" ambition from the 13 Sep plan, restated per student.
- Ratios: SOM is ~0.27% of SAM; SAM is ~8.4% of TAM.
- **Canva:** "teacher aid — app mockups", slide 27 (updated 28 Sep, with speaker notes).
  The rings are labelled GLOBAL / EUROPE / MK and a sources line sits at the bottom.

### What "Europe" means

Europe here means **EU-27 + UK + Switzerland, Norway, Iceland + the Western Balkans.** That
is the part of Europe with one data-protection regime (GDPR, or a GDPR-aligned law like
MK's ЗЗЛП), EU hosting, and national curricula the product can load one country at a time.

Eurostat reference year 2024 unless marked. Deliberately **left out**:

- **Türkiye**: 12.9 M students, which would add ~EUR 116 M. It is a different market and
  regulator, and too big to hide inside a "Europe" number.
- **Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Moldova and the Caucasus**: not addressable now.
- **Kosovo and Liechtenstein**: no comparable 2024 data. Both are small, so the SAM is
  slightly conservative.

**The first step outside MK is the Western Balkans.** Serbia, Albania, Bosnia and
Herzegovina and Montenegro together have ~917,000 students (~EUR 8.3 M a year), about 7× Macedonia,
without Kosovo. Serbian, Bosnian and Montenegrin are mutually intelligible, so one
translation opens three of the four markets.

### Caveats (say them if asked, not on stage)

- **The TAM is priced at the European price.** In low-income countries the realistic price
  is lower, so the global TAM is a ceiling, not a forecast. Lead with the SOM.
- **The buyer is the institution, not the student.** The price is quoted per enrolled
  student and the headmaster or university pays for all of them. Enrolment is the unit
  because it is the one number published the same way at all three levels. School counts
  are not published reliably worldwide.
- **Cross-checks:**
  - MK from the State Statistical Office's own start-of-2024/25 counts: 67,143 secondary +
    53,535 first-cycle tertiary = 120,678 students, or ~EUR 1.09 M. Same headline. Eurostat
    also counts master's and doctoral students, which is why it is higher.
  - World from the UIS 2024 *estimates*: 288.4 M + 268.6 M = 557.0 M, or ~EUR 5.01 B. Same
    headline.
- **EUR 9 per student per year is unvalidated.** The average MK secondary school has ~525
  students (67,143 / 128), which is ~EUR 4,700 a year per school. That is about double the
  old EUR 2,388 flat fee. Logged in [[risks-and-assumptions]] (row 1c).

### How to reproduce the numbers

- **Eurostat:**
  `https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/educ_uoe_enra01?format=JSON&sex=T&worktime=TOTAL&sector=TOT_SEC&isced11=ED3&isced11=ED5&isced11=ED6&isced11=ED7&isced11=ED8`.
  Tertiary is ED5 + ED6 + ED7 + ED8.
- **UNESCO UIS:** `https://api.uis.unesco.org/api/public/data/indicators`. Use indicator
  `20070` (upper secondary enrolment) and `25053` (tertiary, all programmes), with geoUnit
  `SDG: World` for the world and `GBR` for the UK.
- Pulled 28 Sep 2026.

## North Macedonia by institution (State Statistical Office, 2024/25; higher ed 2023-25)

Kept as context for the SOM and for the sales plan. Sales are still counted in schools and
faculties, even though the price is now per student.

| | Institutions | Students | Staff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary (гимназија + стручно + уметничко) — **client** | **128** | **67,143** | **~7,000** (estimate — see note) |
| Higher education (universities) — **client** | **15** | **53,535** (first-cycle, 2024/25) | **4,656** (academic staff, 2023/24) |
| **Client total — secondary + universities** | **143** | **~120,700** | **~11,650** |
| Primary + lower secondary — *not a client, future context only* | 943 | 180,627 | 19,447 |

Note on secondary teachers: the SSO release used for the pitch reports students and schools;
the teacher figure for primary (19,447) is from the same release as reported by the press.
The secondary teacher count is estimated from earlier SSO tables (~7,300–7,500 in the late
2010s) and the reported decade decline of ~5%. **Get the exact 2024/25 figure from the SSO
before quoting it; say "about seven thousand" on stage.**

Trend: students −20% in secondary over ten years, teachers −5%. Class sizes are falling;
schools are consolidating. Nobody is buying tools for growth — they buy for quality and
reputation. That fits the headmaster pitch. It also means per-student revenue in MK shrinks
slowly each year; Europe-wide upper-secondary enrolment is flat to slightly up (EU-27:
18.23 M in 2022 → 18.53 M in 2024).

Note on the 15 universities: several — UKIM alone has 23 faculties — operate as many largely
independent faculties, each a separate sale. Per-student pricing removes the "per
university or per faculty?" counting question from the market size; it stays a sales
question.

## Why Macedonia first — the whole country, from day one

**Decided 13 Sep, still true: the SOM is the whole of Macedonia, not Bitola first.** The
team is based in Bitola, but the sale is national from month one — every private gymnasium
and university in the country is a live target immediately.

- One national curriculum (БРО) — one corpus covers every classroom in the country at once.
- One language, one ministry, one e-register — the whole country is one market, not 34
  separate municipal markets.
- Small enough to reach every headmaster in the country in person within a year.
- **The number to say with confidence is the SOM.** The global TAM is the ceiling on the
  slide, not the claim. Judges believe "a third of Macedonia's secondary schools and
  universities in three years" more than they believe a share of five billion.
