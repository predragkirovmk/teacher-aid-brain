## Design rules for the deck

- Font with proper Cyrillic in case a Macedonian word appears (the three sentences on
  slide 6, the quote on slide 10). Inter, Manrope or Source Sans; avoid decorative fonts.
- Headline ≥ 80 pt, footnotes ≥ 24 pt — the judges sit far away.
- No bullet lists. No stock photos of laughing students. No AI-generated imagery.
- Playful palette on student-side slides (5, 6-left), calm on teacher/headmaster slides
  (6-right, 7, 8) — the same split as the prototype.
- Numbers only where the script says them. Nothing on a slide you do not say out loud.

## Export

- Canva Present mode in the browser tab (no download needed) — the browser also holds the
  prototype tab; switch with Ctrl+Tab (Cmd+Shift+] on Mac).
- Export a PDF too, in case the organizers want a file; keep it on a USB stick and in the
  presenter's email.
