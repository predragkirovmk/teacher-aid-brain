# Startup Weekend Bitola 2026

## Goal

Win the customer-validation and execution scores on Sunday with a live demo the judges
touch, a business model that has numbers in every block, and a five-minute pitch that
explains TeacherAid clearly and makes the room laugh twice.

## Why it matters

The weekend is the first time the idea meets strangers with money and opinions. What
survives Q&A becomes the plan; what does not becomes [[risks-and-assumptions]].
