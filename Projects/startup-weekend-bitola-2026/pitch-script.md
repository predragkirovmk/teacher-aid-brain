# Pitch script — TeacherAid, 5:00

Word for word. Timings are targets; the stopwatch in [[rehearsal]] decides what gets cut.
Stage directions in *italics*. `[NAME]` = your name. `[QUOTE]` = the teacher quote chosen
from [[validation-log]].

Before you walk up: browser tab 1 = the prototype host screen showing the QR
(`/host`, session `BITOLA`); tab 2 = the Canva deck in Present mode on slide 1. Phone in
your pocket, already joined as a backup voter. Printed QR on the table. See [[demo-plan]].

---

## 0:00 — Live opener (~60 s including pauses)

*Projector shows the QR. Do not introduce yourself yet.*

Before I say a single word about our startup — everyone with a phone, judges included,
please scan this. Ten seconds. I'll wait.

*Pause. Watch the "joined" counter. When it stops moving, or at 15 seconds, continue.
Say the number you see.*

Seven in. One question, and it's a real one. **Will Bitola see snow before the first of
December?** Vote — and if you're brave, type one word why.

*Pause ~20 s. Bars grow on the projector. Tap Reveal when the bars settle. Read one reason
out loud, the funniest one.*

Look at that. "[read a reason]." We'll find out in December.

*Tap once more: the mini leaderboard appears. Point at it.*

And there's your leaderboard. You got points for the reason, not for being right — nobody
knows yet.

## 1:00 — Hook (~20 s)

*Switch to the Canva tab. Slide 2.*

You just did the first ninety seconds of a TeacherAid class. Notice what didn't happen:
nobody checked Instagram.

I'm [NAME]. TeacherAid turns the most boring forty-five minutes of a teenager's day into
the one they don't want to miss.

## 1:20 — Problem (~35 s)

*Slide 3 — three characters.*

Three people walk into a Macedonian classroom.

A teacher, who planned last night from a curriculum older than TikTok.

Thirty students, with phones in their pockets and attention that resets every few minutes.

And a headmaster, who isn't in the room — but is judged on what happens in it.

The teacher has a textbook. The students have phones. The headmaster has a speech about
digitalization.

## 1:55 — Solution: the 45 minutes (~65 s)

*Slide 4 — the timeline. Point along it as you go.*

Here's a TeacherAid class, from the bell.

Minute zero: the teacher shows a QR, students scan — attendance done. No roll call.

Minutes one to five: a prediction or a debate, generated by AI from today's topic. You just
did it. Students commit before they know anything. There's twenty years of research on
this — it's called productive failure: guess first, learn second, remember more.

Minutes five to forty: the teacher teaches. Whenever they want — one tap — a question lands
on every phone. Ten to thirty seconds. Points.

And during the lesson, the app blocks the distractions. Instagram, TikTok, games — paused.
The teacher sees who's focused.

Last five minutes: what the class got wrong — and the anonymous questions students sent
during the lesson. The ones nobody asks out loud.

*Slide 5 — leaderboards.*

Points go to a class leaderboard, a school leaderboard, a national one. Schools compete.

## 3:00 — Teacher and headmaster (~35 s)

*Slide 6 — "Three sentences in."*

And the teacher's setup? Three sentences about today's lesson — or upload the plan they
already have. The AI writes the opener, the questions, the timing. In Macedonian. Editable.

*Slide 7 — "Does nothing. Gets everything."*

The headmaster? Does nothing. That's the feature. Every month, a one-page report — lessons
run, attendance, engagement — to forward to parents, to the municipality, to the newspaper.
The most progressive headmaster in town, and they never opened the app.

## 3:35 — Business (~40 s)

*Slide 8 — price. Slide 9 — market.*

Who pays: the school. Five euros per teacher per month. Fifty teachers — three thousand
euros a year. Less than one smart board. First month free.

We start with private gymnasiums: the headmaster owns the budget and decides in a week.
Public schools follow, through the municipalities.

Macedonia has 128 secondary schools, about seven thousand teachers, sixty-seven thousand
students. Small country, one curriculum — the perfect place to prove this.

## 4:15 — Validation and why now (~25 s)

*Slide 10 — quotes.*

This weekend we talked to teachers. One of them told us: "[QUOTE]."

And in May the Minister of Education said it herself: the teacher decides when phones are
used in class. We're the reason to say yes.

*Slide 11 — why now.*

Why now? A generated lesson in Macedonian costs about five cents. Two years ago it cost a
teacher's evening.

## 4:40 — Close (~20 s)

*Slide 12 — the line. Slow down. Look at the judges, not the screen.*

Every class in Macedonia starts the same way: a teacher talking, thirty heads looking down.

We want every class to start with a question.

We're TeacherAid. Thank you.

*Stop. Do not add "any questions". Wait for the moderator.*

## If the demo fails

Say, without apology: "The wifi has opinions. Here's what you would have seen —" and switch
to the Canva tab: slide 2 carries a screenshot of the reveal. Skip the leaderboard line.
Continue from the hook. You lose 20 seconds and nothing else. Full fallbacks in
[[demo-plan#Fallbacks]].
