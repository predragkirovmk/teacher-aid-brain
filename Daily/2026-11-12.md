# 2026-11-12

## Today

- **Teacher app redesign: direction approved, light look.** The owner asked for a simpler,
  calmer teacher app (`/teacher`) with an Apple feel, every feature kept, planned first and
  approved step by step through a link. The plan went onto a private review page and was
  approved the same day (10:10 UTC). Decision, the rules it replaces and the steps:
  [[teacher-app-redesign]].
- Six earlier rules of the teacher app change with it: light instead of dark teal, a teal
  main button with yellow kept for a live lesson, 48px and 44px buttons, rows that open
  instead of a button and a link on every card, 17px body text, and a sidebar that steps
  aside during a live lesson.
- UI libraries are allowed in the teacher app (owner): headless, accessible parts only,
  styled with the app's own tokens.
