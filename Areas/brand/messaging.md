# Messaging

Lines to reuse across the pitch, the deck, the website and the headmaster meeting. Pick,
do not stack — one line per moment.

## The vision line (the close)

**"Every class starts with a question."**

Long form: "Every class in Macedonia starts the same way — a teacher talking and thirty
heads looking down. We want every class to start with a question instead."

## One-liners by audience

- **Judges / general:** "We turn the most boring 45 minutes of a teenager's day into the
  one they don't want to miss."
- **Teacher:** "Three sentences in. A whole lesson out. And the phones work for you."
- **Student:** "Guess first. Being wrong scores."
- **Headmaster:** "Does nothing. Gets everything." / "AI in every classroom — and nothing
  for you to learn."
- **Parent:** "The teacher decides when phones are used. During the lesson, the app blocks
  the distractions. The school owns the data."
- **Against alternatives:** "Everything else is a tool for the teacher or a game for the
  students. TeacherAid is the lesson." / "One tool for the whole lesson, not five."

## Proof lines (say once, with the source ready)

- "Predicting before you learn improves what you remember — even when the prediction is
  wrong." — the pretesting effect and productive failure; see
  [[learning-from-mistakes-research]].
- "Attention in a lecture lapses within minutes and recovers during active tasks." — Bunce
  et al., 2010.
- "As of May 2026 the Minister says the teacher decides when phones are used in class." —
  see [[macedonian-education-system#Phone policy]].
- "A generated lesson costs about five cents." — see [[cost-structure]].
- "128 secondary schools, about seven thousand teachers, 67,143 students." — SSO 2024/25.

## Humor beats (the pitch is fun, not motivational)

- After the live vote: "Congratulations — you just did the first ninety seconds of a
  TeacherAid class. Notice what didn't happen: nobody checked Instagram."
- The three characters: "The teacher has a textbook. The student has a phone. The
  headmaster has a speech about digitalization."
- The headmaster feature: "The headmaster's favourite feature is that there isn't one."
- On being wrong: "In our app, being wrong with a reason scores more than being silent and
  right. Which is also how I got through school."
- On phones: "We didn't invent phones in class. We're just the first thing on them that the
  teacher chose."

Rules: one joke per minute at most; never at the expense of teachers; never at the expense
of a judge who voted wrong.

## Words to avoid

- "Revolutionize", "disrupt", "empower", "gamify" (say "points" and "leaderboard" instead),
  "AI-powered" as a standalone value, "platform", "ecosystem", "solution".
- Never "we will change education". Say what changes at 08:00 on a Tuesday.
