# 2026-10-13

## Decisions

- Installed the `apple-design` skill and applied an Apple HIG pass to the student app:
  three-tab bar with a detached Scan button, no drawer, grouped lists, large titles, a next
  lesson card on Home, system font for body text, a camera pre-prompt, confirm sheets.
  Snapshot of the earlier version kept on branch and tag `app-demo-blue-yellow-v1`.
- Added purposeful motion: check-in lock and draw, attendance count-up, card-to-subject morph,
  physical sheets, notification badge, achievement unlock, smooth FAQ.

  swipe (slow navigation, rapid swipes, early animation end).
- Committed and pushed to `main` (`7f25e35`); the team link is
  https://teacher-aid-bitola.vercel.app/app. Details in [[student-app-demo]].

## Audit of the student app demo (afternoon)

- Tested the live https link with headless Chrome (emulated touch, 375/390/430px, 200% text,
  reduced motion, reduced transparency, increased contrast, fake camera feeds). Not tested on a
  real iPhone or Android phone.
- Main findings: corrupt saved data crashes the app with no way out; 200% text breaks four
  screens; each swipe adds a history entry; a check-in link opened while logged out is lost;
  the demo shows a timetable, a scheduled pop quiz and a streak badge, which
  [[feature-spec-v1]] rules out. Waiting for a go before fixing.
- Marked as partly superseded: [[technical-architecture]] (Flutter, mandatory focus lock),
  [[user-flows]], the magic-link lines in [[risks-and-assumptions]] and
  [[channels-and-relationships]], streaks in [[features]] and [[name-and-mascot]].
  [[feature-spec-v1]] status set to the spec of record.
