# Rehearsal — run of show, timing, cuts

Three runs minimum: alone with a stopwatch, once for the team (they play judges and scan),
once in the room if the organizers allow.

## Timing targets

| Block | Target end | Hard limit |
|---|---|---|
| Live opener | 1:00 | 1:15 — if the room is slow, vote yourself and move |
| Hook | 1:20 | |
| Problem | 1:55 | |
| Solution (45 minutes) | 3:00 | 3:10 |
| Teacher + headmaster | 3:35 | |
| Business | 4:15 | |
| Validation + why now | 4:40 | |
| Close | 5:00 | 5:00 — the moderator will cut you |

Speak at 120–130 words a minute. English is not the room's first language either; slower
is clearer, and pauses read as confidence.

## Cut order if the first run is over 5:00

Cut from the bottom of this list upward until you are at 4:45 on the stopwatch:

1. Slide 11 "A lesson costs 5 cents" — drop the two sentences. (−10 s)
2. The Minister sentence in Validation — keep it for Q&A. (−8 s)
3. "There's twenty years of research on this — it's called productive failure" — shorten to
   "guess first, learn second, remember more". (−6 s)
4. The three "has a..." lines in Problem — keep only "The headmaster has a speech about
   digitalization." (−8 s)
5. The leaderboard line in the demo ("And there's your leaderboard...") — go straight from
   Reveal to the Canva tab. (−10 s)
6. Slide 5 "Class. School. Country." — fold into one sentence: "Points go to a school
   leaderboard — and a national one." (−6 s)

Never cut: the four protected lines in [[pitch-script#Lines to protect (never cut)]].

## Run 1 — alone

- Stopwatch on. Read the script out loud, standing, tab switching included.
- Note the time at each block. Apply the cut order.
- Record it on your phone; listen once for filler words ("so", "basically", "like").

## Run 2 — team as judges

- Teammates scan the real QR on mobile data. One of them deliberately does not scan
  (fallback 1). One asks a hostile question from [[qa-prep]] after.
- Practice the tab switch from `/host` to Canva — it should take one second and no
  comment.
- Practice pressing **S** (simulation) once, mid-demo, so it is a reflex.
- Someone times you; someone watches the judges' faces, not you.

## Run 3 — in the room (if possible)

- Same laptop, same projector, same distance. Reset the session, print the QR, walk to the
  back row and scan.
- Decide the browser zoom for `/host` so the QR fills the height of the screen.

## Stage notes

- Start with the demo instruction before your name — the surprise is the hook.
- After the reveal, take the laugh; do not talk over it.
- Hands: one on the laptop for Space/tab, the other free. Do not hold the phone.
- Look at the judges during the close. The screen has one line; they can read it.
- After "Thank you", stop. Silence is fine. The moderator opens Q&A.

## The night before

- Session reset. Laptop charged. Deck exported to PDF and emailed to yourself. Printed QR
  in the bag. Phone charged, joined as a voter. Script printed in 14 pt, one page, in your
  pocket — you will not read it, but you will know it is there.
