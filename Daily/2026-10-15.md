# 2026-10-15

## Where the product stands

- Reviewed the whole of TeacherAid. The student app demo ([[student-app-demo]]) is polished
  (about 70 commits of design and accessibility work), but every number in it is invented, the
  quiz plays against simulated classmates, and there is no teacher side. The only real
  multi-phone session is still the Startup Weekend opener ([[prototype]]). The
  [[validation-log]] is empty, and the app has never run on a real phone. The Flutter repo has
  not moved since 22 Sep.
- Recommended next build: one real lesson end to end (teacher console, projected class view with
  the QR, real phones answering, End gives the absentee list and the most-missed questions).
  Not chosen yet; the tidy pass below came first.

## Decisions

- **Next build: tidy the demo only.** No new features, and the demo keeps the things
  [[feature-spec-v1]] rules out (timetable, next-lesson card, scheduled quiz, streak badge,
  class 7B).
- **Student identity for the pilot: school email login**, as in [[feature-spec-v1]] 3.2.
  Assumption A (every pilot school issues student mailboxes) still has to be checked.
- **Focus lock in the pilot: a web focus flag, not the native lock.** The app notices when a
  student leaves the quiz during a question and shows a flag on the teacher's console. No app
  store, works on every phone now. This changes [[feature-spec-v1]] 6.1 for the pilot; noted
  there.

## Built (student app, local commits 9b292b8 to e088ce1, not pushed)

- Blue and yellow app icon (the yellow M), replacing the old midnight bell on the home screen.
- Works offline: after one visit with signal, every screen opens and the QR scan checks in with
  no network. Tested with every host blocked.
- Windows high contrast keeps the selected tab, switches, chosen options and card edges.
- Swipes from the right edge are left to the phone (Android back, Safari forward).
- Tapping Back on a subject shrinks the card back into its row on Home.
