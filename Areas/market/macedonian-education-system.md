# The Macedonian education system — who decides, who pays

What a seller of classroom software needs to know. Sources at the bottom.

## Institutions

| Body | Role | Why it matters to TeacherAid |
|---|---|---|
| **МОН** — Ministry of Education and Science | Policy, law, national programs, e-Дневник, the Office 365 agreement for schools | Legitimacy; a possible national buyer years out; the phone-policy voice |
| **БРО** — Bureau for Development of Education | Writes the curricula (наставни програми) per subject and year; teacher training | The curriculum corpus the AI generates inside |
| **ДИЦ** — State Examination Centre | Matura, national testing | Not directly relevant; matura prep packs are a later product |
| **ДПИ** — State Education Inspectorate | Inspects schools | A headmaster's "innovation" story is told here too |
| **Municipalities** | Since decentralization (2005) they found and finance primary and secondary schools from block grants; the mayor appoints headmasters on the school board's proposal (verify current procedure) | **The payer for public schools.** One office per city |
| **Училишен одбор** — school board | Governs the school; proposes the headmaster | Part of the headmaster's audience |
| **Private schools** | Licensed by МОН, own budgets, tuition-funded | The beachhead: the headmaster (or owner) decides alone |

## Secondary education

- Four-year гимназија (general), 2–4-year стручно (vocational), уметничко (arts).
- 128 regular upper secondary schools at the start of 2024/25; 67,143 students; 17,984
  first-year enrollments. Enrollment fell 0.2% year on year, and ~20% over the decade.
- Lesson period: **45 minutes** in secondary (40 in primary).
- Teachers: roughly 7,000 in upper secondary (estimate; the decade trend is −5%; get the
  exact figure from the SSO release before it goes on a slide).
- Private secondary schools: around a dozen, mostly Skopje (verify count and list; Bitola
  has few or none — the first private school may need to be in Skopje).

## Primary and lower secondary (later market)

- 943 schools, 180,627 pupils, 19,447 teachers (2024/25). Nine years. Younger students,
  fewer phones, and any phone restriction lands here first.

## Phone policy — the current line

On 11 May 2026 Minister Vesna Janevska said a ban on phones during lessons in secondary
schools "will be discussed", and described the current practice: many schools collect phones
during lessons and return them at the break, and **the teacher decides** — "Доколку
наставникот процени дека е потребно да се употреби мобилниот телефон на децата, тогаш тој ќе
даде посебна дозвола." She also framed educational use as a legitimate reason, noting that
not every school has internet, smart boards and tablets. Municipalities, as the bodies
responsible for schools, can set their own rules.

What this means for TeacherAid:
- There is **no national ban** as of September 2026. The rule is teacher's discretion.
- If a ban comes, it will almost certainly carve out teacher-sanctioned educational use —
  the Minister already described that exception. TeacherAid is the sanctioned use.
- The secondary wedge is the safer one; a primary-school restriction is more likely.
- Keep the quote ready for Q&A. Do not lead with it in the pitch — use it as the answer.

## e-Дневник and EDUINO

- **e-Дневник**: the national electronic class register (attendance, grades, remarks).
  Official attendance lives here. TeacherAid does not replace it; sync is roadmap.
- **EDUINO**: the national digital education platform launched in 2020 with UNICEF and
  partners — video lessons, games, open resources. A content library, not a live classroom
  tool. Partner, not competitor.
- **Office 365 / Teams**: schools have had Microsoft accounts through МОН since the COVID
  years. Teams is for distribution and remote lessons; nothing for the 45 minutes in the room.

## Money

- Public schools: block grants from the state to municipalities, then to schools. A
  headmaster has very little discretionary budget. Purchases go through the municipality and
  the public procurement law; small-value procurements can be direct (verify the current
  threshold in EUR and whether software subscriptions qualify).
- Private schools: tuition, own budget, decide in days.
- Teacher net salary: roughly EUR 600–700 / month (verify) — a useful anchor for "EUR 3,000 a
  year for a whole school".

## Bitola specifically

- Seven public secondary schools (gymnasium, technical, economic, medical, and others —
  verify the list and headmaster names before outreach), under the Municipality of Bitola.
- UKLO (St. Kliment Ohridski University) has a Faculty of Education in Bitola — the natural
  research partner.
- Startup Weekend Bitola is itself a door: mentors, the municipality, local press.
