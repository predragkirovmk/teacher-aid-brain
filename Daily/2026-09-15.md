# 2026-09-15

Two days after the pitch. Spent the day turning pitch-depth feature notes into a build spec.

## Today

- **Wrote [[feature-spec-v1]]** — the v1 build specification, developer-facing, 15 sections.
  Produced from a structured decision pass rather than written from the existing notes: every
  feature decided deliberately, including a lot the pitch never had to answer.
- Decisions that **supersede** what the older notes said:
  - **Focus lock is mandatory** — no authorization, no join. Replaces "not blocked, only
    flagged". The student takes the no-phone path instead: present, no points, never absent.
  - **Anonymous questions are anonymous to classmates, not to the teacher.** The fear being
    designed around is peer judgement. Side effect: no anonymous channel for abuse.
  - **Per-topic correctness is stored per student.** Assessment data on a minor — needs its
    own ДПА clause and changes the "never grades anyone" line.
  - **Optional practice sets after class.** Crosses the "no homework / not an LMS" line the
    vault drew four times.
  - **The e-учебник unit is the planning input**, not three typed sentences. Its rights are a
    blocking legal item with МОН/БРО.
  - **One-tap refinements replace the teacher chatbot.**
  - **School-email login for students**, so nobody can play as someone else.
  - **No timetable at all.** A teacher prepares a lesson whenever and runs it whenever.
  - **Universities and practical lessons are out of v1.**
- New in the spec, absent from every earlier note: accounts and roles, a school admin role,
  question types beyond multiple choice, images and formulas, running one lesson across
  parallel classes, the school lesson library, the two-window projector split, roster
  lifecycle, offline answer queueing, notifications, accessibility, staff access control.
- Six assumptions the spec rests on went into [[risks-and-assumptions]] as rows 0–0f. The
  largest is that schools issue student mailboxes — without it the login design fails.
