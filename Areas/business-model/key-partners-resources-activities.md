## Key partners — who helps deliver the model

| Partner | What we want from them | What they get | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **МОН / БРО** (Ministry; Bureau for Development of Education) | Legitimacy; curriculum programs in structured form; eventually a national-program buyer | A digital tool aligned with their programs; data on engagement | Not contacted. The Minister's May 2026 line — the teacher decides on phones — is our opening. |
| **Municipality of Bitola** (education department) | Payer for public schools; introduction to the seven public secondary schools; a pilot they can publicize | "First municipality with AI in classrooms" | Not contacted. After the first private school. |
| **UKLO — Faculty of Education, Bitola; teacher associations** | An advising teacher; a small study on the opener's effect (productive failure in MK classrooms); credibility with МОН | Research material; a modern tool for their students | Not contacted. The education mentor at SW is the first step. |
| **Telecom (A1, Makedonski Telekom)** | CSR sponsorship of public-school seats; possibly zero-rated data for the student app | "AI in Bitola's schools" with their name; the mobile data students use | Not contacted. Year-2 conversation. |
| **AI provider (Anthropic)** | Startup credits; reliability; Macedonian quality | A visible education case | Apply for the startup program after the weekend. |
| **EDUINO** (national platform, МОН + UNICEF) | Integration or endorsement; not a competitor — a content library, not a live tool | Live classroom layer on top of their content | Not contacted. |
| **A private school (the first)** | The pilot; the first report; the referral | First-mover story, free month, a say in the roadmap | Warm contact, no yes yet — see [[startup-weekend-bitola-2026]]. |

Partners reduce two risks: **legitimacy** (МОН, UKLO) and **payment** (municipality,
telecom, grants).

## Key resources — what is essential

- **The AI lesson engine**: prompts, structured output, the 45-minute frame, Macedonian
  quality. Cheap to start, valuable once tuned by hundreds of teacher edits.
- **The MK curriculum corpus**: БРО programs loaded per subject and year, then the growing
  body of teacher-edited lessons and questions. This is the asset that compounds and the
  thing a foreign competitor would have to rebuild.
- **The live-session engine**: QR join, points, timers, focus lock — the native student app
  is the hard part.
- **The team**: 4+ people at Startup Weekend. Still missing as of 16 Sep 2026: a teacher,
  and a mobile developer for the focus lock — the latter is the first paid role.
- **An advising teacher**: still unfilled, and the oldest open item in the vault. Every claim
  about classrooms needs one person who has stood in front of thirty 16-year-olds.
- **AI credits and hosting**: the only cash cost in month 1.
- **Trust artefacts**: the ДПА template, the privacy notice, the monthly report sample.

Removed from v1: "headmaster" — a customer, not a resource.

## Key activities — what we must do exceptionally well

1. **Build** the planner, the live session, the dashboard, the report. In that order.
2. **Curate** curriculum content per subject and year so generation stays inside what is
   taught.
3. **Sell** to headmasters: the meeting, the free month, the report sample. Run free live
   sessions where they open a door.
4. **Onboard and support** teachers so the first lesson takes ten minutes and the login
   never fails at 08:05.
5. **Run the game**: leaderboard seasons, rewards, the school-vs-school layer.
6. **Report** monthly, automatically, to every headmaster.
