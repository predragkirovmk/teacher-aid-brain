# Outreach texts that bring links

Links from other sites are what teacheraid.education lacks most ([[ranking-plan]]). These texts
make it easy for a school, an organiser or a journalist to write about us **and link to us**.
Every one asks for a link to `teacheraid.education`, not just the name: the name alone is read
as "teacher aide".

Rules:
- Send only to people we actually know or have worked with. Never buy links or swap them in bulk.
- Fill every `[bracket]`. Remove anything that is not true for that school yet.
- Point people to the press page for logos, screenshots and facts:
  `teacheraid.education/za-mediumi` (live once app PR #20 is merged).
- Log every text sent in [[validation-log]] or the day's note: who, when, and whether a link
  appeared.

## 1. A news post the school publishes on its own site

After a free lesson. Send it to the director with text 2. School sites carry weight with Google.

> **Час со TeacherAid во [име на училиштето]**
>
> На [датум] учениците од [паралелка] имаа час по [предмет] со апликацијата TeacherAid. Наставникот
> [име на наставникот] ги испраќаше прашањата од лаптопот, а учениците одговараа на своите
> телефони. Присуството се запиша со едно скенирање на QR-код на таблата.
>
> TeacherAid е апликација за часот, направена во Битола, за средните училишта и факултетите во
> Македонија. Повеќе на [teacheraid.education](https://teacheraid.education).
