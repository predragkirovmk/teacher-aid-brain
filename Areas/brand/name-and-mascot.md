# Name and mascot

## Working name: TeacherAid

Used everywhere this weekend — docs, prototype, script. Do not change it on Saturday night
unless deck, prototype and script are all updated together (the prototype name is one
variable in `content/opener.json`).

Why it is not great:
- Generic and descriptive; "teacher's aide" is a job title; hard to own as a mark.
- "Aid" reads as first aid / charity in English.
- Names the teacher, but the loudest channel is students and the buyer is the headmaster.
- Says nothing about the lesson, the game, or Macedonia.

Why it is fine for Sunday: judges understand it instantly, and instant beats clever in a
five-minute pitch.

## Alternatives, ranked

| Name | Meaning | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zvono / Ѕвоно** | "the bell" | The bell starts every lesson and the game. One word in Macedonian, Serbian, Bosnian, Croatian, Montenegrin — the whole expansion map. Mascot-ready. Warm, school-universal, nothing to explain. | Common noun; check .mk/.com availability and existing marks in the region. |
| **Chas / Час** | "the lesson period" (also "hour") | The unit of the product. Shortest possible. Very Macedonian. | Generic word; hard to search; "Chas" reads as a name in English. |
| **Predvidi / Предвиди** | "predict" (imperative) | Names the core mechanic and the pedagogy. | Long; only the opener, not the whole lesson; harder abroad. |
| **Kreda / Креда** | "chalk" | Classic, pan-Balkan, one word. | Retro; says "old school". |
| **Prvi5 / Првите 5** | "the first five (minutes)" | Memorable hook. | Names one feature; the product is 45 minutes. |

**Recommendation:** Zvono, with the mascot below. Decide after the weekend, not before.

## Mascot (idea only today)

Concept: **Zvonko** — a small school bell with a face, named after the common name Zvonko
("the one who rings"). Roles:
- The face on the one-tap refinement row ("Zvonko, make it harder"). There is no chatbot in
  v1 — see [[feature-spec-v1]] 4.6 — so the persona attaches to the buttons, not a
  conversation.
- The little character on student screens: rings for a pop-up, shrugs at a wrong answer,
  celebrates a badge. Never shames — the app is on the student's side.
- The mark on the QR screen and the monthly report footer.

Design direction (when there is time): flat, two-colour, rounded, readable at 24 px on a
phone and at 2 m on a projector. No 3D, no gradients, no AI-generated look.

If the name stays TeacherAid, Zvonko still works as the mascot — a bell fits any classroom
product.

## Checklist before committing to a name

- [ ] .mk and .com (or .app) domain available
- [ ] No existing edtech product with the name in the region (search Google, App Store,
      Play Store, Serbian and Croatian too)
- [ ] Trademark search at ДЗИС (State Office of Industrial Property)
- [ ] Says well in Macedonian and English on stage
- [ ] Works as an app icon
