# 2026-10-12

## Decisions

- **New design system, version 2: blue and yellow.** The five new student screens replace the
  midnight-glass identity of 24 Sep. Kept the blue and yellow core, deepened the blue so white
  text passes contrast, one yellow accent. Full rules in [[design-system]]. The old system is
  retired and archived in `teacher-aid-flutter/design/teacheraid/_archive/v1-midnight-glass/`.
- Recommendations given before building: contrast on the light cards, labelled navigation with
  Scan in the centre, glass only for overlays, a subject colour system, real week data,
  bilingual copy, no flags for languages.
- Built the student app demo at `/app` in the `teacher-aid-app` repo, with a working QR
  scanner ([[student-app-demo]]). Deployed; Vercel deployment protection was switched off on
  the `teacher-aid` project so the team can open previews without a login.
- Stack choice: a PWA inside the Next.js repo, not the Flutter project, to share a link.
